How this prototype works

1. Enter your nickname and a signal’s entry, stop, targets and leverage.

2. The calculator estimates risk-based position size and P&L scenarios.

3. Save the signal, then use Record exit to log the price you actually closed at and the portion of the position closed.

4. Export your personal journal as CSV for backup.

Multi-user requirement: for everyone on the team to sign in and see their own journal from any device, this static prototype needs a backend (authentication + database + access rules). Local browser storage alone cannot share records or safely provide real accounts.
